Reimbursement note

Employer-based reimbursement, insurance, or HSA/FSA/HRA programs apply to certified birth & postpartum doula support only—not NCS. You remain fully responsible for fees; payment at signing is your non-refundable retainer regardless of reimbursement outcomes. Bring written benefit documentation to your consult if you plan to seek reimbursement.

Dense contract rules (late payment, early termination, backups) are summarized on the Services page accordion and detailed in your written agreement.
