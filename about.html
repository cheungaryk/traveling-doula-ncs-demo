About Cjaisey

Private Service Professional · Certified Labor, Postpartum & Infant Care Doula · Newborn Care Specialist · Family Support Professional · Community Educator

A steady presence for growing families

With more than 25 years supporting children, families, and households, Cjaisey Williams provides comprehensive private family support grounded in professionalism, thoughtful stewardship, and collaborative care.

As a Certified Birth & Postpartum Doula and Newborn Care Specialist, she walks with families through pregnancy, postpartum recovery, newborn and sibling adjustment, and early childhood—with evidence-based education and individualized support.

Her background spans non-medical doula care, newborn care, childcare from newborn through high school, household operations, vendor coordination, domestic and international travel support, and special-needs advocacy. She has worked in private residences, high-profile and ultra-high-net-worth households, and underserved communities—bringing discretion, emotional intelligence, and adaptability to every partnership.

Cjaisey supports short- and long-term live-in and live-out roles across the U.S., with travel as needed. When aligned, short-term newborn care can grow into a long-term ROTA or weekend live-in nanny partnership built on clear expectations and mutual respect.
