Contract & payment details

The essentials live here so the offerings above stay scannable. Full terms appear in your service agreement.

Preferred Client Rebate (private-pay)

Pay the contract in full at signing and receive a 3% Preferred Client Rebate via Zelle during the final scheduled week of services. Applies only to successfully completed private-pay contracts—not canceled, early-terminated, or materially modified agreements.

Reimbursement, HSA/FSA & insurance

Employer reimbursement, health insurance, or HSA/FSA/HRA programs apply to certified birth & postpartum doula support only—not NCS support. You remain 100% financially responsible; full payment is due at signing as a non-refundable retainer regardless of reimbursement outcomes.

To use these programs, provide written documentation confirming provider approval (named or general certified doula), covered services, Superbill requirements, and any Letter of Medical Necessity needs.

Payment timing & default (short-term contracts)

Payment schedules are outlined in your agreement. Invoices are due upon receipt. For long-term relocation contracts where the calendar is blocked exclusively, unpaid invoices within 12 hours of receipt may place the client in default with automatic termination, acceleration of the remaining balance, and forfeiture of amounts previously paid—including retainer, completed hours, travel fees, and food allowances.

Early client termination (other than documented breach by the contractor) typically leaves the client responsible for 100% of the remaining contract balance plus return travel expenses.

Backups during postpartum contracts

Your contract secures Cjaisey’s personal schedule. If illness or emergency requires a backup postpartum doula or NCS, that backup is paid directly by the client at their independent rate—you are never billed by Traveling Doula NCS for those covered shifts.
